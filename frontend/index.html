Total Maritime Dependency

India imports 88.2% of its crude oil requirements (5.405 MBPD).

Every day, dozens of supertankers traverse contested international waterways from the Persian Gulf, Red Sea, West Africa, and Russia to feed 23 domestic refineries.

Chokepoint Concentration

48.1% of all Indian crude sits behind a single 21-mile strait.

2.598 MBPD flows exclusively through the Strait of Hormuz from Iraq, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait, and Qatar. Another 43.6% relies on Bab-el-Mandeb and the Red Sea corridor.

The Razor-Thin Strategic Buffer

India holds only 9.5 days of Strategic Petroleum Reserves (SPR).

Compared to the IEA 90-day standard or China’s 80+ days, a prolonged blockade in the Persian Gulf exhausts sovereign emergency stocks in under two weeks.

KrudeAi Neural Architecture

Fine-tuned domain weights served locally via KrudeAi inference engine.

Training Provenance & Limitations

  • Model: KrudeAi Domain-Adapted Intelligence Model
  • Dataset: 1,512 verified maritime headlines → 1,313 train / 199 holdout
  • Inference Input: Raw breaking news headline string (no corridor leak at inference)
  • Serving Stack: High-throughput local runtime, q8_0 quantization, CUDA acceleration
Known Architectural Trade-off: Model was trained on headline + corridor + event type, but infers from headline alone in production to support un-categorized breaking news.

Terms & Conditions

1. Purpose: Krude is an open-source energy security intelligence digital twin designed for geopolitical risk evaluation, crude maritime supply routing, and sovereign Strategic Petroleum Reserve (ISPRL) scenario modeling.

2. Empirical Models: Risk indices, freight calculations, and LP optimization outputs are calibrated from public maritime datasets (PPAC, Vortexa, AIS, GDELT, and NewsAPI) for research and decision-support.

3. Open Source: Developed under the MIT license for academic and empirical supply chain research.

Privacy Policy

1. Data Collection: Krude does not collect, sell, or monetize any personal telemetry or tracking cookies.

2. Local Inference: Custom inference sandbox requests run locally or against specified local endpoints without remote data logging.

3. Third-Party Feeds: Live maritime news feeds are retrieved securely using authenticated public news APIs directly from your configured environment.
